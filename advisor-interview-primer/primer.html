The Four Pillars of Personal Finance

Most client conversations come back to these four topics. Know each one in a sentence, and know the order they come in.

  1. 1Save build the cushion
  2. 2Protect manage risk
  3. 3Invest grow wealth
  4. 4Keep more reduce taxes
1 · Savings & account types

Match the account to when you need the money

The sooner you need it, the safer and more accessible the account should be.

TodayChecking
Within a yearHigh-yield savings, money market
1–5 yearsCDs, Treasury bills
Retirement401(k), Traditional IRA, Roth IRA
HSA · health costs 529 · education Brokerage · any goal
  • Emergency fund first: 3–6 months of expenses in savings.
  • Free money: always get the full employer 401(k) match.
2 · Managing risk

Protect the plan before you grow it

You can't avoid risk, but you can cushion it, transfer it, and spread it out.

DiversifySpread it out, don't bet on one company
InsureTransfer big losses: health, life, disability, home, auto
Emergency fundCushion small shocks without selling investments
  • Risk tolerance is how much loss a client can stomach. Risk capacity is how much they can afford. Go with the lower one.
  • Time horizon: the longer until the money is needed, the more risk the client can take.
3 · Investing

Higher expected return comes with bigger swings

Stocks mean you own a piece of a company. Bonds mean you lend money for interest. Funds hold a basket of both.

Approximate long-run average annual U.S. returns, rounded. Past results don't guarantee future returns.
  • Asset allocation, the stock/bond mix, drives most of a portfolio's results. Rebalance to keep that mix on target.
  • Time + compounding: by the Rule of 72, money earning 7% doubles in about 10 years.
4 · Taxes

It's not what you earn, it's what you keep

Every dollar sits in one of three tax buckets. What matters is when the tax is paid.

TaxableTax as you goBrokerage, savings account
Tax-deferredTax laterTraditional 401(k), Traditional IRA
Tax-freeTax now, not laterRoth IRA, Roth 401(k), HSA for medical
  • Brackets are marginal: only the dollars above each threshold are taxed at the higher rate.
  • Hold investments over 1 year for lower long-term capital gains rates.
  • A deduction lowers taxable income. A credit cuts the tax bill dollar for dollar.
