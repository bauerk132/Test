# Precalculus MAT201 — Master Curriculum Suite (Modules 5–9)

A comprehensive, unified Precalculus application built with **React 19**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**. This platform provides a cohesive curriculum mesh spanning **Modules 5 through 9**:
- **Module 5 (Polynomial & Power Functions)**: 5 Exam Questions
- **Module 6 (Rational & Radical Functions)**: 5 Exam Questions
- **Module 7 (Transformations & Symmetry)**: 10 Total Exam Questions + 5 Scaffolded Walkthroughs
- **Module 8 (Exponential Functions)**: 10 Total Exam Questions + 5 Scaffolded Walkthroughs
- **Module 9 (Logarithmic Functions)**: 10 Total Exam Questions + 5 Scaffolded Walkthroughs

**Total Content**: **40 Authentic Exam Questions** + **15 Step-by-Step Guided Walkthroughs** with instant grading, tiered hints, and partial credit scratchpad.

---

## 🎯 Unified Curriculum Mesh

### 📐 Module 5: Polynomial & Power Functions
- **5 Exam Problems**:
  - Quadratic vertex optimization ($h = -b/(2a)$, maximum profit models).
  - Leading term test and polynomial end behavior.
  - Zeros, multiplicity, and graph behavior (touching vs. crossing).
  - Synthetic division, polynomial division, and Remainder Theorem ($R = P(c)$).
  - Rational Zero Theorem, factoring, and finding real and complex conjugate zeros.

### ⚡ Module 6: Rational & Radical Functions
- **5 Exam Problems**:
  - Domain restrictions, non-canceling vertical asymptotes ($x = c$), and removable holes $(c, f(c))$.
  - Horizontal asymptotes and slant (oblique) asymptotes via polynomial long division.
  - Solving rational equations with extraneous root detection ($x \neq \text{restrictions}$).
  - Inverse functions ($f^{-1}(x)$) and domain/range restrictions.
  - Solving radical equations with squaring and checking for extraneous solutions.

### 🔄 Module 7: Transformations & Symmetry
- **5 Guided Walkthroughs + 10 Exam Questions**:
  - Vertical and horizontal shifts $f(x \pm h) \pm k$.
  - Reflection across axes $-f(x)$ and $f(-x)$.
  - Vertical and horizontal stretches and compressions.
  - Composite point coordinate mapping: $(x, y) \to (x/b + h, a \cdot y + k)$.
  - Rigorous algebraic proofs of even ($f(-x) = f(x)$) and odd ($f(-x) = -f(x)$) symmetry.
  - Transformed domain and range interval boundaries.

### 📈 Module 8: Exponential Functions
- **5 Guided Walkthroughs + 10 Exam Questions**:
  - Base-$b$ and natural exponential graphs and horizontal asymptote mapping ($y = k$).
  - Solving exponential equations with common bases ($b^u = b^v \implies u = v$).
  - Solving unlike-base exponential equations using natural logarithms ($a^{f(x)} = b^{g(x)}$).
  - Periodic compounding ($A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$) vs. continuous compounding ($A = Pe^{rt}$).
  - Radioactive half-life decay models ($Q(t) = Q_0(1/2)^{t/h} = Q_0 e^{kt}$) and population growth.

### 🧮 Module 9: Logarithmic Functions
- **5 Guided Walkthroughs + 10 Exam Questions**:
  - Logarithmic definition and inverse relationship ($\log_b(x) = y \iff b^y = x$).
  - Argument domain positivity restrictions ($\text{arg} > 0$) and vertical asymptotes.
  - Product, quotient, and power expanding and condensing laws.
  - Solving multi-logarithmic equations and discarding non-positive extraneous roots.
  - Logarithmic ratio scales (Decibels $dB = 10\log_{10}(I/I_0)$ and Richter magnitude).

## 🚀 Key Interactive Features

- **Thematic, Classy & Colorful Bold UI**: High-contrast, dark jewel-toned aesthetic with color-coded badges, glowing borders, and clean typography.
- **Scaffolded Walkthroughs**: Active verification at each milestone with multi-tiered hints.
- **"Show Your Work" Scratchpad**: Smart regex and algebraic verification that grants partial credit for valid intermediate mathematical steps.
- **Interactive Function Sandbox**: Dynamic SVG visualizer with real-time curve rendering for quadratic, absolute value, exponential, and logarithmic functions.
- **Master Formula Reference Modal**: Complete reference sheet covering formulas for Modules 5 through 9.
- **Diagnostic Gap Engine**: Post-exam diagnostic breakdown that categorizes missed questions by micro-skill and generates a personalized study plan.
- **GitHub Sync**: One-click modal to commit and push changes or export the repository.
