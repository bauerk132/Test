# Example: an invented dorm desk corner

This shows both turns: the question, then the plan. The room is made up.

## Turn 1: the question

> I see a dorm desk corner: clothes, a gym bag, and papers on the floor, and a crowded desk top.
>
> **How long do you want to spend cleaning?** 15 minutes · 30 minutes · 1 hour · 2+ hours

The user answers **30 minutes**.

## Turn 2: the plan

### What I see
A desk corner used for studying. Most of the mess is on the floor between the desk and the door, and the desk top is crowded.

- **Floor:** gym bag, hoodie, 3 socks, 2 empty water bottles, a pizza box, a stack of papers, a phone charger cable across the walkway
- **Desk top:** mug, headphones, textbook, loose pens, a medication bottle with the cap off
- **Under the desk:** a shoe box (closed), a power strip
- **Chair:** jacket
- **Existing storage:** desk drawer, a half-empty shelf above the desk, a hook on the back of the door

### Do first (safety)
- **Cap the medication bottle** and put it in the desk drawer. Open medication spills and is easy for pets to reach.
- **Move the charger cable out of the walkway.** It's a trip hazard, and stepping on it damages the cable.

### The five piles
- **Trash / recycle:** pizza box, 2 water bottles
- **Clothes:** hoodie and socks to the hamper; jacket to the door hook
- **Belongs elsewhere:** mug, to the kitchen
- **Keep here:** gym bag, papers, textbook, pens, headphones, charger cable, medication bottle, power strip
- **Ask first:** the shoe box. I can't see what's in it.

### Zones and homes
| Zone | Home | What goes there | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Go | Floor beside the door, under the hook | Gym bag | You grab it on the way out |
| Study | Shelf above the desk | Papers (in one folder), textbook | Within reach from the chair, and it keeps the desk top clear |
| Tech | Back corner of the desk, cable run along the wall | Headphones, charger cable | Out of the walkway and near the outlet |
| Personal | Desk drawer | Medication, pens | Closed, and in the same spot every time |

The power strip stays under the desk, where it's already out of the way.

### Order of work (30 minutes)
1. Safety: 3 min (total 3)
2. Trash: 3 min (total 6)
3. Clothes: 4 min (total 10)
4. Mug to the kitchen: 2 min (total 12)
5. Floor items to their homes, gym bag first: 10 min (total 22)

That leaves about 8 minutes spare. **Safe to stop after step 1**: after that, nothing risky is left out.

### Keep it that way
- The gym bag goes by the door as you walk in.
- Two-minute desk reset before bed: mugs out, papers in the folder.

### What I couldn't tell
- What's in the shoe box.
- Whether the hoodie and socks are clean. I assumed they're worn.
